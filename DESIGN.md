## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial luxury narrative tailored for high-end civil engineering, structural development, and turnkey architecture spanning dual-terrain geography: the bustling commercial hub of the plains (Siliguri) and the dramatic, slope-engineered terrains of the Eastern Himalayas (Darjeeling and Kalimpong).

The visual aesthetic balances **Editorial Architectural Luxury** and **Modern Precision Minimalism**. It rejects the cluttered, blue-collar tropes typical of traditional construction websites, opting instead for the refined sophistication of an architectural monograph coupled with tactile glassmorphism. It evokes:
- **Authority & Endurance:** Deep, structural emerald greens echoing Himalayan forests and eternal permanence.
- **Craft & Energy:** Warm construction amber and sunset ochre signifying heavy machinery, brass architectural accents, and warmth in cold climates.
- **Clarity & Air:** Pristine, warm-paper whites that elevate architectural photography, floorplans, and engineering deliverables.

The experience speaks to HNIs, commercial real estate developers, hospitality groups, and homeowners seeking turnkey excellence, earthquake-resilient hill engineering, and immaculate contemporary finishes.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural intersection of dense mountain pine, heavy earthworks, and warm domestic light pouring from floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass.

- **Primary (`#0D6B63` / `#064E46`):** Deep Mountain Emerald. Conveys structural permanence, engineering integrity, and sustainable construction. Used for key headlines on light backgrounds, hero structural badges, active navigation indicators, and primary corporate framing.
- **Secondary (`#F59A23` / `#E07A08`):** Construction Saffron / Rich Ochre. Represents high-visibility precision, raw kinetic power, and bespoke luxury interior lighting. Used for primary call-to-actions, highlight text accents, architectural milestone metrics, and focused interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#0B1B18` / `#0F2420`):** Charcoal Obsidian & Dark Forest. Used for deep-contrast architectural evening spreads, footer engineering zones, process roadmaps, and full-bleed hero overlays.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FCFCF9` / `#F8F9FA`):** Warm Alabaster & Crisp Off-White. Mimics premium heavy-stock architectural paper, eliminating clinical digital glare and emphasizing high-resolution project photography.
- **Border & Glass Outlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)` / `rgba(13, 107, 99, 0.12)`):** Used to delineate frosted metric overlays, project cards, and structural grid dividers.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an editorial conversation between classical monumentality and contemporary Scandinavian functionality:

- **Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Selected for its commanding high-contrast serifs, sharp finials, and luxury editorial weight. It grounds page headers, editorial intros, and client quotes with the gravitas of architectural heritage. It should never be tracked out; letter spacing remains tight (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`).
- **Body, UI, & Engineering Data (`Manrope`):** Selected for its geometric structure, wide apertures, and pristine clarity at scale. It handles complex bills of quantities, engineering bullet points, navigation bars, and operational workflows.
- **Editorial Sub-Headings & Handscript Touches:** Selected accents employ soft italics to indicate human craft ("From Concept to Creation", "People. Projects. Possibilities.").
- **Uppercase Kicker Labels (`label-caps`):** Used consistently over major section headlines to introduce subject categories (`OUR SERVICES`, `FEATURED PROJECTS`, `PREMIUM CONSTRUCTION`) with expanded letter spacing (`0.12em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system capped at a maximum width of `1320px` to maintain architectural editorial balance on ultra-wide desktop displays.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `4rem` outer canvas padding. Section dividers utilize breathing room (`space-3xl` / `6rem`) to let project photography dominate.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Complex dual-pane grids (e.g., About Us story + photo) reflow into stacked modules with reduced inter-section spacing.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Horizontal service matrices and project galleries convert to smooth horizontal scroll carousels with peek cues or single-column vertical stacks.
- **Rhythm & Structure:** Alternating section backgrounds (Warm Alabaster `#FCFCF9` vs. Obsidian Dark `#0B1B18`) denote structural shifts between public portfolio viewing, internal technical processes, and quotation conversions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines physical material surfaces with luxury glassmorphic metrics:

1. **Backdrop Glassmorphism (Metrics & Navigation):**
   - Floating hero counters and persistent headers leverage `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with semi-transparent tinted backgrounds (`rgba(11, 27, 24, 0.65)` on dark backdrops, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` on light backdrops).
   - Edges are framed with hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` or `rgba(13, 107, 99, 0.08)`), imparting an architectural drafting ruler feel.

2. **Ambient Layered Shadows:**
   - **Card Rest State:** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 27, 24, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 27, 24, 0.03)`.
   - **Card Hover State:** `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(11, 27, 24, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 27, 24, 0.04)`. Card translates upward by `-4px` to communicate physical lift.
   - **Button Focus Glow:** Soft amber ambient glow (`0 8px 20px -2px rgba(245, 154, 35, 0.35)`).

3. **Tonal Planar Sections:**
   - Rather than relying solely on drop-shadows to define hierarchy, sections shift between `#FCFCF9` paper canvas, `#F0F4F2` architectural light gray, and deep `#0B1B18` structural earthworks.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, low-curvature philosophy (`roundedness: 1` / Soft) reflecting the sharp geometry of precision architectural drafting, structural I-beams, and masonry blockwork.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for micro-badges, form tags, and technical indicator pills.
- **Component Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for standard buttons, project thumbnail cards, service tiles, and input containers.
- **Editorial Cards & Modals (`0.75rem` / `12px`):** Used for large feature cards, testimonials, and glassmorphic stat modules.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Reserved strictly for carousel arrow controls, filter category tabs (`All`, `Residential`, `Commercial`), and circular play buttons on project videos.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Construction Orange):** Solid `#F59A23` background with white `#FFFFFF` text. Border radius `8px`. Padding: `12px 24px`. Hover: shifts to rich amber `#E07A08` with an accompanying subtle rightward micro-motion (`2px`) on adjacent arrow icons.
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA (Architectural Emerald):** Outlined with `1.5px solid #0D6B63`, emerald text, transparent background. Hover: background fills with `rgba(13, 107, 99, 0.06)`.
- **Dark Section Hero Pill:** Transparent backdrop with `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`, white text, housing play button icons or secondary project anchors.

### Service Grid Tiles
- Soft ivory background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle border (`1px solid #EAECE8`).
- Centered or left-aligned architectural line-art icons housed within an amber-tinted or emerald-tinted circle container (`44px` x `44px`).
- Bold modern title in Manrope with a 2-line engineering summary below. Hover elevates the card with an orange base border accent (`2px solid #F59A23`).

### Project Portfolio Showcase Cards
- Full-bleed 16:10 aspect ratio imagery featuring warm golden hour or moody hill architecture.
- Gradient scrim (`linear-gradient(180deg, transparent 40%, rgba(11, 27, 24, 0.9) 100%)`) along the base.
- Title in `Playfair Display` overlaid with location badge icon (`Siliguri`, `Darjeeling`, or `Kalimpong`) and directional arrow trigger on the bottom right.

### Step-by-Step Process Timeline
- Linear track connected via amber milestone nodes (`01`, `02`, `03`... `08`).
- Active and completed states use solid amber `#F59A23` circular rings with crisp bold typography; upcoming states use muted emerald-slate lines (`#2A423D`).

### Filter Chips
- Capsule shapes (`rounded-full`) with `8px 18px` padding.
- Active: solid dark slate (`#0B1B18`) or primary emerald (`#0D6B63`) with white text.
- Inactive: transparent background with soft gray border (`#DCE0DC`) and muted gray-slate text (`#4A5A56`).

### Input Fields & Consultation Form Elements
- High-contrast structured fields. Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F9FA`.
- Hairline border `1px solid #CED4CE`. Active focus switches to `1.5px solid #0D6B63` accompanied by zero-spread ambient ring (`rgba(13, 107, 99, 0.15)`).
- Typography: crisp 14px Manrope with high-contrast neutral placeholders (`#7A8884`).
